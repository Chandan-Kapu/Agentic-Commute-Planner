Help to use

Plan your commute in 5 steps.

Enter where you start, where you’re going and when you need to arrive. The agent checks live traffic, BART, Caltrain, ACE and weather, tells you when to leave and how to go, and alerts you if that changes.

Requiredneeded to planOptionalimproves the plan

1Sign in Optional · recommended

Click Sign in (top right), enter your email and open the link you receive.

  • You get: settings saved on any device, plus automatic 9 PM plans and morning alerts.
  • Without sign-in: everything still works, but it’s saved only in this browser and you press the buttons yourself.

2Your commute

FieldNeeded?What to enter
Start addressRequiredWhere you leave from, e.g. Mountain House Pkwy, Mountain House, CA
Destination addressRequiredWhere you’re going, e.g. Great America Pkwy, Santa Clara, CA
Arrive byRequirede.g. 9:00 AM
ArrivalRequiredHard deadline = must be on time · Flexible = OK to arrive a bit later to avoid traffic (preset)
Earliest / Latest I’d leaveRequiredYour departure window, e.g. 7:00–8:00 AM. Earliest must be before latest.
WorkdaysOptionalThe days you commute (preset Mon–Fri)
Drive the whole wayOptionalTurn on to compare driving; add your main freeway and parking cost if you know them

3Your stations

Turn on at least one way to travel: Drive, BART, Caltrain or ACE. Required For each train you turn on:

FieldNeeded?Notes
Board at / Get off atRequiredPick from the list; the two must be different
ACE train timesRequired for ACEMorning times at your station, e.g. 06:15, 07:20 (see acerail.com)
Drive to station (min)RecommendedYour usual drive to the station
Ride (min)Auto-filledChange it if you know better
Walk / shuttle to destinationRecommendedMinutes from the last station to your door

4Alerts Optional

Open Alerts & memory under Your stations.

  • Alert if plan shifts by: how many minutes of change should trigger an alert (preset 10).
  • Alert webhook: to get alerts on your phone, install the ntfy app, subscribe to a private topic and paste https://ntfy.sh/your-topic.

5Get your plan

  • Save & plan tomorrow: the night-before plan.
  • Save & re-plan now: uses live conditions this morning and shows what changed.
  • Pretend it’s … (top of the page): try a morning re-plan at any time of day.

What you get

  • ✅ Best way to goLeave time, arrival time, chance of being late and cost.
  • 🔁 Alternativese.g. drive vs. Dublin BART vs. ACE vs. waiting until traffic eases.
  • 🤖 Why it chose thatThe AI agent shows which data it checked and how it decided.
  • 🟢 Live data statusGreen = live, amber = sample data, red = source unavailable.
  • 🔔 Alertse.g. “I-580 is backing up. Leave 15 min early, or take ACE.”
  • 🧠 Learns youPress I took this and future plans lean toward the modes you actually use.

Tips

  • Address not found? Add the city and “CA”.
  • A plan takes about 10 seconds while the AI agent checks the data.
  • This is a planning aid, not a safety system. Always check conditions yourself.
